QUICK TOOLS

Before you take action.

A few simple steps can help you prepare before raising a concern.

01

Understand the issue

Write down what happened and identify the specific concern you want to address.

02

Check the rules

Review your institution's official policies and relevant procedures.

03

Keep records

Keep copies of relevant communications, documents, dates, and other information.

04

Find appropriate support

Consider speaking with an appropriate student service, adviser, or responsible authority.

i

Important information

Resources on this website are intended for general educational and informational purposes. Student rights and procedures can vary depending on your location, institution, and applicable laws. Always consult official policies and appropriate professionals for specific situations.
