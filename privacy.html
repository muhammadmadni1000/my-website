Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use this website.

Last updated: August 2026
ON THIS PAGE Information We Collect How We Use Information Information Sharing Cookies Data Security Your Rights Contact Us
01

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with the Student Rights Campaign website, such as when you submit a contact form, join our community, or subscribe to updates.

This information may include your name, email address, institution, and any information you choose to include in your message.

02

How We Use Information

Information provided through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested information, manage communications, and improve our website and activities.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

03

Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, comply with applicable legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of individuals and the organization.

04

Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand how visitors use the website, and improve the user experience.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information held about you. These may include requesting access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

If you have a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

07

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact the Student Rights Campaign team.

Email: hello@studentrightscampaign.org
