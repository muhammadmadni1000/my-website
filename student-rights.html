YOUR FOUNDATION

Every student deserves a fair opportunity to learn.

Student rights can vary depending on your country, institution, and applicable laws. The principles below provide a starting point for understanding the areas where students commonly have important rights and protections.

Always check your institution's official policies and applicable local laws for specific guidance.

01

Academic Rights

Students should be able to understand the academic rules and requirements that apply to them.

  • Clear academic policies
  • Transparent assessment processes
  • Access to relevant academic information
  • Fair consideration of academic concerns
02

Freedom of Expression

Students may have protections relating to expressing views and participating in discussion, subject to applicable laws and institutional rules.

03

Privacy

Learn what information your institution collects, how it is used, and what policies govern access to student records.

04

Equal Treatment

Students deserve fair treatment and appropriate protections under applicable anti-discrimination laws and policies.

05

Due Process

If you face disciplinary or academic action, learn about the procedures, notices, hearings, appeals, and other protections available to you.

06

Safe Learning

A respectful educational environment should support student safety, dignity, and access to appropriate help when problems arise.
